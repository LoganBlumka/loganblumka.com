Space Trash

Space Trash is a serious game developed by the student researchers of CSERG and the Husky Game Design (HGD) Enterprise that's goal is to recreate the Object Hit and Avoid (OHA) task on the KINARM to collect data outside of lab, allowing data to be collected through the online community.
