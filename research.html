Space Trash

Space Trash, a project started by Dr. Leo Ureel II from the Department of Computer Science, and Brandon Woolman from the Department of Cognitive Science, is a game that aims to re-create the Object Hit and Avoid (OHA) task on the Kinseological Instrument for Normal and Altered Reaching Movement (KINARM), which collects visuomotor data to help aid in Alzheimers Disease (AD) research.

The reason why we are trying to re-create this task is because the KINARM has two flaws. The first being that it's only available at institutes like R1 colleges, and with only 150 being reportedly made, that means that there aren't many available. Secondly, if you where wondering why they wouldn't just make more of these instruments, it's because it cost around 50,000-250,000$ to re-create it, depending on what model you need.

Space Trash aims to re-create this task, gamifying and packaging it for use through the online community, allowing us to collect data around the world.

Another thing too note is that for the first time ever, Husky Game Design has partnered with a researching group to bring Space Trash to the enterprise. With the enterprises support, we will be able to increase production speed on this game, allowing us to test our game sooner and get this packaged out at an earlier date!

Any papers produced from this project will be on the document page, as well, you can learn more about this on our official Space Trash page for CSERG, as well as is free to reach out to me at anytime!
