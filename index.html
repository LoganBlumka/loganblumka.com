About Me

I'm a undergraduate software engineering student at Michigan Technological University researching into how Natural Language Processing models can be used to improve fields like customer service, education and security.

I come from a small town in Michigan called Buchanan, where I lived most of my life. During my Junior year of high school, I learned that I had a passion for programming, wanting to pursue a career in software engineering not knowing what field that I wanted to focus on

After arriving at tech and taking a couple years to explore my options, developing projects here and there, I found that I had a keen interest in Natural Language Processing applications and how they can be used to improve applications.

On other tabs of this website, you can learn more about some of the specific projects that I'm working on, as well as my current research with the Computer Science Education Research Group (CSERG), my personal research into Natural Language Processing, and there road map on planned updates!
